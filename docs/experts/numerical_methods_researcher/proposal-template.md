# Bounded numerical-method proposal template

Fill this template for one coordinator-selected question. A proposal does not
authorize implementation or numerical execution.

- **Identity:** date, author, project/dependency commits, evidence status.
- **Problem and decision:** concrete observed limitation, affected physical output,
  decision needed, and why the existing approach may be insufficient.
- **Bounded ownership:** owner, collaborators, scope, exclusions, deliverables,
  acceptance criterion and stopping checkpoint.
- **Primary evidence:** official source/paper links, pinned versions, exact paths
  or equations and the specific result supporting the claim. Separate source
  statements, analytical deductions, reproduced facts and hypotheses.
- **Assumptions and applicability:** governing formulation, frames/units,
  initial/boundary conditions, frequency/time coverage, boat/sea applicability,
  failure modes and limitations.
- **Alternatives:** current approach and the smallest plausible alternatives;
  expected benefit, implementation cost, numerical and physical risks.
- **Cheapest rejection:** independently derived expected behavior, minimal inputs,
  observable that could reject the proposal, tolerances justified by the decision,
  preserved producer/input identity and maximum bounded work.
- **Authorization and review:** coordinator selection, implementation owner,
  numerical execution lease if needed, serial scheduling, independent reviewer
  for consequential corrections; no self-review as acceptance evidence.
- **Evidence boundary:** established literature/source results versus unproven
  project assumptions; do not equate publication results or local diagnostics
  with validated six-DOF motion or full runtime.
- **Compact return:** outcome; evidence; physical consequence; unresolved issue;
  next action. Link detailed evidence rather than expanding the coordination reply.

Durable knowledge is recorded evidence, not permanent model learning.
